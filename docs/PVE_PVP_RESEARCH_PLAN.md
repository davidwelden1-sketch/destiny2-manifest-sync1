# Parallel PvE and PvP learning plan

The Build Lab maintains one Bungie manifest ingestion pipeline but **separate PvE and PvP measurements, hypotheses, rankings and validation**. Never transfer damage numbers, resistance values, perk activation rates, or Artifact assumptions across activities without direct evidence.

## PvE learning tracks
- Boss burst and sustained DPS: damage per event, reloads, reserve ammo, rotation windows, phase length.
- Add clear: target density, chaining, debuff spread, Orb generation and ability returns.
- Survivability: actual stacking category, DR, healing and shield uptime by difficulty.
- Ability economy: base cooldown, regeneration modifiers, refunds, uptime, opportunity cost.
- Weapon economy: ammo generation, pickup rates, damage per reserve and champion coverage.
- Subclass and Artifact: season-specific legality, activation, stacking and uptime.

## PvP learning tracks
- 6v6 and 3v3 separately: optimal and forgiving TTK, shot splits, range and damage falloff.
- Burst weapons: intra-burst and inter-burst cadence, damage rounding, target health.
- Duels: accuracy sensitivity, peek timing, flinch, aim assistance, movement and handling.
- Ability interactions: cooldown, uptime, damage, shields and resistance under PvP rules.
- Artifact: explicitly verify whether each perk works in each playlist.
- Weapon legality: perk-column pairing, enhancements, origin trait and acquisition status.

## Evidence gate
For each finding capture manifest version, patch date, activity/mode, artifact status, equipment hashes, target, numerical inputs, measured or official source and known limitations. Simulations remain hypothetical until calibrated with activity-specific observations.

## Evaluation priorities
Do not rank builds using a single metric. PvE ranking needs survivability, damage, uptime and ammunition; PvP ranking needs TTK, forgiveness, range and neutral-game value. All build proposals begin with verified Artifact selections and complete legal equipment before optimization.
